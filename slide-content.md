## 5 · Proposed solution: how it works
- Eyebrow: Proposed solution · How it works
- Title: Sign in. Hear it. **Reply. See it signed.**
- Deaf signer → kotoSay → hearing person:
  1. Signs: Signs naturally, with hands and face
  2. Reads: Reads the signs **and** the facial expression
  3. Hears: A natural voice, with the emotion kept
- Hearing person → kotoSay → Deaf signer:
  4. Replies: Types a reply in text
  5. Rewrites: Reorders the reply into **sign grammar**
  6. Sees: A 3D avatar signs the reply back
- Where sign AI failed before → what kotoSay does (WFD & WASLI, 2018):
  - Word-for-word → Reorders into sign grammar
  - Ignores the face → Reads expressions, keeps the emotion
  - One-way only → Two-way: voice out, sign back
