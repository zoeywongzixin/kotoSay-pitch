Social issue · When no one can interpret

A bank. A police station. Both turned a Deaf person away.

Banking · Japan

Instead of providing translation, a bank refused to let a Deaf customer open an account alone.

Source: DPI Japan, via Japan’s FSA (2026)

Police · Yokohama
